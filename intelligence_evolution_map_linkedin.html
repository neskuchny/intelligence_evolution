Intelligence Evolution Map

Public version of your methodology: 10 layers of intellectual-system development, a map of humans / AI / ants, an interactive bottleneck model, company battle mode, and short theses instead of awkward copyable paragraphs.
Core thesis
AGI is synchronization
Not one record metric, but the alignment of memory, reasoning, agency, self-correction, embodiment, and metacognition.
Main bottleneck
Memory + metacognition
Reasoning has already grown quickly. The next shift goes through accumulated experience and knowledge of one’s limits.
Most provocative conclusion
AGI may not be built by one company
The path increasingly looks like an assembly: OpenAI is stronger in reasoning, Anthropic in agents and memory, and DeepMind in embodiment and world-model direction.
What this is
What we built here
We assembled our own methodology for evaluating intellectual systems: not by one metric, but by layers of development — from energy and perception to memory, world models, metacognition, and integration.
1. Derived 10 layers.
This is a framework that helps compare not only AI models, but also humans, ant colonies, the immune system, companies, and organizations.
2. Built a transition map.
Which layer follows which, what has to mature, which bottleneck slows the system, and which next shift is most likely.
3. Turned it into forecasts.
Who is more likely to lead in memory, agents, world models, and metacognition, and why AGI may arrive as an assembly of the best parts.
Which hypotheses
What the model stands on
Hypothesis 1. Intelligence develops in layers, not on a single scale.
Hypothesis 2. Transitions follow an order: memory does not become stable without action and repetition, and metacognition does not emerge without memory and self-checking.
Hypothesis 3. AGI is not a record on one metric, but the synchronization of several mature layers.
Hypothesis 4. Different systems have different ceilings: humans reach almost L10, AI is still around L7–L8, and ant colonies stop below symbolic reflection.

Key answers
Where are humans?
In this model, humans sit around L9: language, abstraction, reflection, and partial metacognition are already present. Reaching L10 is not automatic; it comes through integration.
Where will AI overtake humans first?
On the language-symbolic layer. AI is already natively multilingual, scales knowledge faster, and may reach “L10 of language” before humans do.
Why do ants not reach the upper layers?
Because the colony has coordination and memory in the environment, but no stable internal symbolic model and no metacognition. In this framework, an ant colony reaches roughly an L6–L7 ceiling.
Why does this matter for AI?
AI without memory and self-checking may end up as “a very powerful ant colony”: efficient, but not reflective. That means the transition to AGI requires not only power, but an internal model of itself.
Key roadmap principle
Metacognition must mature last
Why you cannot “turn it up” before memory and self-correction
In this framework, metacognition is not just another capability. It is a model of all the other capabilities: to know what it does not know, estimate confidence, choose a thinking strategy, and inhibit action when evidence is insufficient.
If you give it too early
you get a distorted system: it seems to “reflect,” but it has nothing to stand on. Without mature memory, self-correction, and reasoning, such metacognition is pseudo-metacognition — elegant, but crippled.
Analogy with the brain
The PFC matures last. In humans this is the longest stretch — from adolescence to stable adult regulation. That is why in our model metacognition is the slowest parameter.
Practical implication
AGI is determined not by the best parameter, but by the slowest mature layer. Right now the longest tail remains metacognition, not reasoning.
What AGI means in our framework
Not a super-genius, but a mature intelligence
AGI = all key parameters above threshold at the same time
That means the system remembers, reasons, acts, corrects itself, is grounded in the physics of its environment, and knows the limits of its own knowledge. Not “the fastest,” but coherent and reliable.
Analogy with humans
AGI in this picture is closer to a “25-year-old” than to a sage or a super-genius: fully formed, capable of independent work, but still without deep accumulated experience.
Why the 2032–2035 window still makes sense
Not because reasoning itself will take that long to grow. Rather because after memory and self-correction, the system still needs a stage where metacognition becomes structural and stable rather than decorative.
In short
In this methodology, AGI = S_hard ≈ 1.0, when all key layers are above threshold at the same time.
What ASI means in our view
Not one super-brain, but a swarm of AGI agents
The next level after AGI is not “one even stronger individual intelligence,” but cooperation: specialized AGI agents assemble into a next-order system.
Main inversion
Individual AI → collective AI. As cells became an organism, neurons became a brain, and humans became society.
So ASI = not speed, but wisdom
The highest trait here is not “answer faster,” but knowing when not to act. In other words, not merely maximizing a goal, but understanding the limits of optimization.
Working window
Within your model, this naturally maps to 2035–2040: first mature AGI, then the integration of AGI agents into a higher collective layer.
Where do humans fit here?
Humans do not disappear; they change roles
Humans do not “die” when ASI appears
Just as a neuron did not die when the brain appeared but became part of it, humans in this picture are not automatically replaced — they become a type of node in a new system.
Scenario 1. Technology assembly
Reasoning from one lab, agents and memory from another, embodiment from a third. The most “evolutionary” path.
Scenario 2. Human + AI
A new layer on top of humans: interfaces, assistants, and later perhaps neurointerfaces. Not replacement, but augmentation.
Scenario 3. One winner
Theoretically possible, but right now it explains the split leadership by layers worse than the assembly scenario.
